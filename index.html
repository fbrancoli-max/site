Fernando Brancoli

Associate Professor of International Relations

I am an Associate Professor at the Institute for International Relations and Defense (IRID) at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ). My research focuses on transnational political violence, international security, and climate policy, with particular emphasis on Latin America and the Global South.

I was a Fellow at the Institute for Advanced Study at Princeton (2021-2023). I also teach at COPPEAD Business School and serve as a recurring professor at Fundacao Getulio Vargas (FGV) MBA programs in International Relations.

Research

My research combines academic rigor with policy engagement. I focus on understanding the dynamics of political violence, security challenges, and international cooperation in the contemporary world.

Research Areas

  • Transnational Political Violence — Examining patterns and dynamics of political violence across borders, with focus on non-state actors and their global networks.
  • International Security — Analyzing contemporary security challenges, defense policies, and strategic dynamics in the Global South.
  • Climate Policy — Investigating the intersection of climate change, security, and international cooperation in developing nations.
  • Political Risk Analysis — Providing frameworks for understanding and assessing political risks in Latin America and emerging markets.

Current Projects

I am currently working on projects examining the intersection of climate politics and security in Brazil, as well as research on transnational dimensions of political violence in Latin America.
