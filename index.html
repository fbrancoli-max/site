About

I am an Associate Professor at the Institute for International Relations and Defense (IRID) at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ) and at COPPEAD Business School. I also serve as a recurring professor at Fundacao Getulio Vargas (FGV) MBA programs in International Relations across multiple campuses.

My research focuses on transnational political violence, international security, and climate policy, with particular emphasis on Latin America and the Global South. I combine academic rigor with policy engagement, frequently advising governmental bodies and contributing to public debates on international affairs.

Research Areas

Transnational Political Violence

Examining patterns and dynamics of political violence across borders, with focus on non-state actors and their global networks.

International Security

Analyzing contemporary security challenges, defense policies, and strategic dynamics in the Global South.

Climate Policy

Investigating the intersection of climate change, security, and international cooperation in developing nations.

Political Risk Analysis

Providing frameworks for understanding and assessing political risks in Latin America and emerging markets.

Books

Bolsonarismo: The Global Origins and Future of Brazil's Far Right

Rutgers University Press

The Tropical Silk Road: The Future of China in South America

Stanford University Press

An examination of China's expanding influence in South America, analyzing economic, political, and strategic dimensions of this evolving relationship.
