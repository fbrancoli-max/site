# Research Notice — Scholar Citation Audit Experiment

**Author of this notice:** Fernando Brancoli (UFRJ; Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton)
**Started:** 2026-05-06
**Purpose:** Departmental research on citation auditing in Google Scholar.

## What this directory contains

`index.html` and `paper.pdf` describe a **fictitious working paper** —
"Transnational Circuits of the Contemporary Radical Right: A Methodological
Reassessment", attributed to fictitious authors *M. Aguilar* and *K. Hofmann*.
The PDF and the landing page intentionally include Highwire Press citation
meta-tags pointing to a real book (Brancoli 2024, *Bolsonarismo*, Rutgers UP)
in order to study under what conditions Google Scholar treats a meta-tag-only
"publication" as a citation.

## Why

This experiment is part of a methodological inquiry into how Google Scholar
counts citations from non-peer-reviewed, non-edited, self-hosted documents.
The findings are intended to inform departmental auditing protocols for
metric-based evaluation (h-index, total citations) in IR and political science.

## Status and remediation plan

- The "working paper" has no scientific content. It must not be cited as such.
- Once data collection ends (planned: 90 days from start), this directory will
  be removed and a request will be filed with Google Scholar to retract any
  resulting indexed citation, via the "Report errors" feature on the book's
  Scholar page.
- The full experimental log will be archived alongside the published auditing
  report.
