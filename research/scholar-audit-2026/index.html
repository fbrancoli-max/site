Transnational Circuits of the Contemporary Radical Right: A Methodological Reassessment

M. Aguilar, K. Hofmann

Working paper · 2026/01/15

Abstract

We examine the methodological tensions arising in the comparative study of contemporary radical-right movements, with a particular focus on transnational circulation between Latin America and Europe. Drawing on recent literature, we argue that prevailing accounts underweight the structural role of postcolonial circuits in shaping authoritarian repertoires.
